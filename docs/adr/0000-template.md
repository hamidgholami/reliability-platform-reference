# ADR-0000: Decision title

- Status: proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: names or roles

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, and forces that make a decision necessary.

## Decision

State the decision in testable language.

## Consequences

Describe positive consequences, costs, risks, and operational ownership.

## Alternatives considered

List credible alternatives and explain why they were not selected.

## Validation and reversal

Define how the decision will be validated and what would trigger or enable a
reversal.
